## Your first review

1. Stage a change — `git add`, or the `+` in Source Control
2. Wait about two seconds; the status bar shows **Reviewing…**
3. Open the shield icon in the activity bar

Or skip the staging: right-click any source file → **CR-Track: Review This File**.

Findings appear as squiggles and as rows in the panel, each with accept and
reject. Nothing is changed without you clicking.
